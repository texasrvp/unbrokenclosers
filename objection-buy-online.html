“I'll just buy it online and have it delivered.”

How to handle it, in plain words you can use today. Then practice it until it’s automatic.

Car sales

What It Usually Means

They like the convenience and want to skip the dealership hassle. They often haven't thought about the test drive, the trade or service after the sale.

What Not to Say

Trashing online sellers. It sounds defensive and pushes them toward the screen.

Handle It in Three Steps

  1. Agree that plenty of people buy online.
  2. Recommend they drive the exact vehicle first.
  3. Give them three questions to ask any seller: trade value, return policy and who services it.

Words You Can Use

Any industry

“Thank you so much for sharing. Hey, give it a shot. Before you click buy, drive the one you want, because you can't feel it on a screen. And ask them what they'll give for your trade, what happens if you don't like it, and who works on it later.”

These are the words LJ uses on the floor. Make them yours: the structure matters more than the script. Thank them, ask a couple of questions, answer the real reason.

Practice It Now

An AI customer says “I'll just buy one online and have it delivered.” and keeps pushing until you handle it. Five minutes, scored.
