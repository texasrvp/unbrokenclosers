Coaching, not counseling

Unbroken Closers coaches sales skills and mindset. It is not counseling, therapy or medical care, and it is never a substitute for them.

Where coaching stops

LJ's story includes real hurt, and the Unbroken Assessment can bring up things that run deeper than sales: grief, trauma, a hard childhood, addiction, depression or thoughts of giving up on more than a job.

Those deserve a licensed professional. A coach helps you sell through the hard days. A counselor helps you heal from what's behind them. The strongest people LJ knows have used both.

  • Coaching works on skills, habits, goals and how you show up on the floor.
  • Counseling works on mental health, trauma, grief and relationships.
  • If a coaching conversation goes somewhere it shouldn't, LJ will pause it and point you to the right help.
  • Nothing on this site diagnoses or treats any condition.

Help, any time

  • In crisisCall or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Free, 24/7.
  • VeteransCall 988 and press 1, or text 838255, for the Veterans Crisis Line.
  • EmergencyCall 911.
  • Finding a counselorThe SAMHSA National Helpline, 1-800-662-4357, offers free, confidential referrals 24/7.
  • Faith-basedMany churches offer pastoral counseling or can refer you to a Christian counselor.
