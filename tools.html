Practice and lead tools

The tools behind the coaching. Members use them between sessions so lessons turn into habits.

Included in the Team Program and Closer's Circle

Soft Pitch

An AI customer your reps can practice on any time. It pushes back with real objections, like price, the trade and "I need to talk to my spouse," then scores the response and suggests what to say next.

Managers see who practiced and how they scored on the weekly scorecard.

Bonus for Closer's Circle members

Lead finder

Reps who sell for themselves, like insurance agents, need people to talk to. The lead finder pulls fresh, public buying signals from around the web and scores them, so members have someone to use their new skills on.
