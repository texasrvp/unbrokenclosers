“Just give me your best price.”

How to handle it, in plain words you can use today. Then practice it until it’s automatic.

Any industry

What It Usually Means

They want to skip the dance and not feel taken advantage of. Many buyers expect to be lowballed and want to protect themselves.

What Not to Say

Throwing out a number before you know what they want. You’ll negotiate against yourself.

Handle It in Three Steps

  1. Agree that you’ll be straightforward.
  2. Confirm exactly what they want first, so the price means something.
  3. Give a clear, honest number with what’s included, and explain it.

Words You Can Use

Any industry

“Thank you so much for sharing. I appreciate a straight shooter. You wouldn’t buy it until it was the best deal for you anyway, would you? Perfect, we’re on the same page. Let me make sure I’m pricing exactly what you want, so the number is real and not a teaser. Then I’ll walk you through every line.”

These are the words LJ uses on the floor. Make them yours: the structure matters more than the script. Thank them, ask a couple of questions, answer the real reason.

Practice It Now

An AI customer says “Just give me your best price, I don’t have all day.” and keeps pushing until you handle it. Five minutes, scored.
