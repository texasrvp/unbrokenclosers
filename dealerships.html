Questions

Do You Work With Franchise Stores or Only Independents?

Both. The sweet spot is a store or small group with 5 to 25 salespeople, where the general manager doesn’t have time to run training every week.

Will This Conflict With Our Manufacturer Training?

No. Manufacturer training covers product and process. Unbroken Closers covers the conversation: greetings, discovery, objections and closing, practiced every month.

Can You Train Our BDC or Internet Team Too?

Yes. Phone and follow-up skills are part of the program, and Soft Pitch scenarios can be built for appointment setting.
