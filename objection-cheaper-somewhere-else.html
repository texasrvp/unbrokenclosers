“I can get it cheaper somewhere else.”

How to handle it, in plain words you can use today. Then practice it until it’s automatic.

Any industry

What It Usually Means

They want the best deal and want you to know they’ve shopped. Sometimes the other offer is real and identical; often it’s different in ways they haven’t noticed.

What Not to Say

Trashing the competitor or claiming they hide fees. It sounds defensive and makes you look like the one with something to hide.

Handle It in Three Steps

  1. Thank them for shopping. Smart buyers compare.
  2. Ask to see or understand the other offer so you’re comparing the same thing.
  3. Show the differences that matter to them, and be honest if theirs really is better on price.

Words You Can Use

Any industry

“Thank you so much for sharing. I really do appreciate that. Can I ask you a couple of questions, to make sure you get the best price wherever you buy? Let’s put the two side by side, apples to apples.”

On the car lot

“I don’t know what that price is, so let’s set it aside. If you buy it online, how will you get it insured and shipped? What’s their policy if it shows up with damage? Are you out your deposit? And how does their warranty compare? I’d be happy to help you look that up.”

In insurance

“Glad you’re comparing. Let’s line up the coverage limits and deductibles apples to apples. A lower premium sometimes means less protection when you need it.”

These are the words LJ uses on the floor. Make them yours: the structure matters more than the script. Thank them, ask a couple of questions, answer the real reason.
