The 30-Day Ramp Plan for New Hires

Most new reps quit before they ever feel competent. Every Team Program client gets this plan for each new hire.

  1. Week 1

    Shadow the top rep. Learn the product and the process. Practice the greeting in Soft Pitch every day.

  2. Week 2

    Take ups with a closer nearby. Learn the 5 objections. First scored practice rounds.

  3. Week 3

    Work the floor solo. Daily huddle drills. A mid-ramp check-in with the manager.

  4. Week 4

    First full week on their own. Set a 60-day goal. Recognized in front of the team.

Questions Managers Ask

How Much Does Coaching Cost?

The free workshop costs nothing. The Team Program starts at $1,200 a month for teams of 5 to 15 reps. The Closer’s Circle is $79 a month per rep, bootcamps start at $1,500 and Manager Coaching is $500 a month.

Is This Counseling or Therapy?

No. Unbroken Closers is sales and performance coaching. LJ draws on his own story to help reps get past what holds them back on the floor, but coaching is not counseling, therapy or medical care. When something runs deeper, like grief, trauma, addiction or depression, LJ will point you to a licensed counselor.

Is There a Contract?

The Team Program starts with a three-month commitment, because real habits take that long to form. After that it’s month to month and you can cancel any time. The Closer’s Circle is month to month from day one.

We Already Have an AI Training Tool. Do We Still Need This?

Probably, yes. Most teams buy a practice tool and stop using it within a few weeks because nobody follows up. The Team Program is the follow-up: a coach, a scorecard and recognition that make practice a habit. Your reps can keep using your tool, or use Soft Pitch.

Isn’t Everyone Buying Online Now?

They research online, but most still close in person. Only about 5% of recent car buyers did the whole purchase online. What changed is that buyers walk in informed, which means your reps need better skills, not fewer.

Do You Come to Us?

Yes, on site across Central Texas, including the Killeen, Temple, Waco, Round Rock and Austin area. Teams anywhere else get the same program by video.

When Are Trainings Held?

Usually at your morning meeting before the doors open, or at lunch by video. Closer’s Circle calls are on Tuesday evenings.

What If It Isn’t Working?

The Weekly Scorecard shows practice and progress, so you’ll know quickly. After the first three months the Team Program is month to month.

Do You Work With Industries Other Than Car Sales?

Yes. The program fits any team that sells face to face or by phone: insurance agencies, home services, solar and roofing, and media sales.
