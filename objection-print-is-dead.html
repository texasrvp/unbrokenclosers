“Nobody reads print anymore.”

How to handle it, in plain words you can use today. Then practice it until it’s automatic.

Media sales

What It Usually Means

They've heard that digital replaced print and assume their customers don't read magazines. Personal, local content still gets read.

What Not to Say

Arguing print versus digital. It isn't a contest they care about. They care about being seen by local customers.

Handle It in Three Steps

  1. Ask permission to offer an opinion.
  2. Point out that people keep magazines with their neighbors, kids or church in them.
  3. Show the distribution numbers before they spend anything.

Words You Can Use

Any industry

“Thank you so much for sharing. Would you care if I offered an opinion? When's the last time you threw away a magazine with someone you know in it? It's about showing up where your neighbors already pay attention.”

These are the words LJ uses on the floor. Make them yours: the structure matters more than the script. Thank them, ask a couple of questions, answer the real reason.
