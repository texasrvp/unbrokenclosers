Team Readiness Scorecard

Ten questions about how your team really runs. Get a score, your three biggest gaps and exactly how to fix them.

10 questions · about 3 minutes · answer for your team as it is today

1New reps follow a written plan for their first 30 days instead of shadowing whoever is free.
2Reps roleplay real customer conversations at least once a week.
3Every rep answers the five most common objections the same, proven way.
4Every customer who leaves without buying gets a follow-up plan within 24 hours.
5Each rep can tell you their own closing ratio, appointments and units for the month.
6Every rep gets a one-on-one coaching conversation with a manager every week.
7Top performers are recognized in front of the team every month with something they can keep.
8The team starts each day with a short, focused huddle that trains one skill.
9Most new reps are still on the team after 90 days.
10After a slow week, the team gets back to work instead of complaining or coasting.
