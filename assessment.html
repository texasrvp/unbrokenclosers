Sixteen Inner Objections, One Life Story

Each inner objection comes from a chapter of LJ’s memoir, Stripped Bare, But Unbroken. He’s lived every one of them, and he coaches from that.

The Scarcity Voice

From the chapter: Why Do We Live Here?

Discomfort asking for money, because money always felt like it was for other people.

The Outsider Voice

From the chapter: Birth Defects

Feeling judged before you speak, and shrinking in front of confident buyers.

The Comparison Trap

From the chapter: Sibling Rivalry

Measuring your worth against the top rep instead of your own last week.

Taking No Personally

From the chapter: Another New School

Hearing every no as a verdict on you, so you stop asking.

Nobody Showed Up for Me

From the chapter: Parents · Grandparent Gold

Going it alone because the people who should have shown up didn’t.

The Mood Swing

From the chapter: Grandparent Gold · I Joined the Military

Effort that rises and falls with how you feel that day.

Lost Your Why

From the chapter: Stay Hungry · I’m a New Dad

Coasting, because the reason you started has gone quiet.

Quitting When It Hurts

From the chapter: Toughness · Keep Going

One bad week and you’re browsing job listings.

Waiting for Better Soil

From the chapter: Landing on Earth

Grow where you’re planted, instead of blaming the store, the leads or the market.

The Blame Game

From the chapter: Thank You, Tom Greene

Taking responsibility for you, instead of waiting for someone else to fix it.

The Wrong Circle

From the chapter: Swimming with Salespeople

Iron sharpens iron. You become like the people you spend your day with.

The Commission Love Affair

From the chapter: The Real Cost of Love

Loving the commission more than the customer, and losing the career it could build.

Promises That Slip

From the chapter: More Caught Than Taught

Do what you say you’ll do for your customers, every time.

Just Another Up

From the chapter: A Special Customer

We all need each other. Every customer is a person first.

Selling Yourself Short

From the chapter: A Better Version of Me

You are better than you think, and your goals should say so.

Flying Blind

From the chapter: The Road Less Travelled

Most people quit too soon and never track their progress.

This is a coaching tool built from LJ’s story, not a psychological test or a diagnosis. Unbroken Closers coaches sales skills and mindset. When something runs deeper, like grief, trauma, addiction or depression, the right person is a licensed counselor, and LJ will tell you so. Find support.
