“I'll just raise my deductible.”

How to handle it, in plain words you can use today. Then practice it until it’s automatic.

Insurance

What It Usually Means

They want a lower bill and the deductible is the easiest lever they know. It can be smart, or it can leave them stuck after a claim.

What Not to Say

Talking them out of it automatically. For some clients a higher deductible is the right call.

Handle It in Three Steps

  1. Agree it can be a smart move.
  2. Ask whether they could pay the full deductible tomorrow without strain.
  3. If not, look at other savings first: discounts, bundling and the coverage they carry.

Words You Can Use

Any industry

“Thank you so much for sharing. That can be smart. If you had a claim tomorrow, could you cover a thousand dollars without it hurting? If not, let's look at other ways to save first.”

These are the words LJ uses on the floor. Make them yours: the structure matters more than the script. Thank them, ask a couple of questions, answer the real reason.

Practice It Now

An AI customer says “I'll just raise my deductible to lower my payment.” and keeps pushing until you handle it. Five minutes, scored.
