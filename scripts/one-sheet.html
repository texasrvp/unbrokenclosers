About LJ

LJ learned selling the hard way: on a car lot, in finance and insurance offices and knocking on doors. He has recruited, trained and developed sales teams across multiple states, and founded Unbroken Closers to give face-to-face sales teams coaching that lasts past the pep talk.

“Sales is a lot like life: you get told no, you get knocked down, and the people who win are the ones who show up again the next morning.”
